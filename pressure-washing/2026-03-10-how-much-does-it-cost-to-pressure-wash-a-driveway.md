Staying within budget for pressure washing a house or driveway can be a bit of a challenge. Referring to some commonly asked questions and answers about pressure washing a driveway will make it easier to hire the best professional for the job.

Q.Can you damage concrete with a power washer?

Yes. Concrete can be damaged by a power washer or pressure washer if the pressure is too high, the concrete is less than a year old, or if the concrete is power washed more than once a year. A driveway power washing professional can help ensure the correct pressure is used to minimize damage.

Q.What’s the difference between pressure washing and power washing?

They both use highly pressurized water to clean, but a power washer uses hot water while a pressure washer doesn’t.

Q. How long does it take to pressure wash a driveway?

It typically takes about 30 minutes to pressure wash a driveway, depending on the size. If the driveway can accommodate two or more vehicles or is extra long, it will take more time. Using one of the best pressure washer surface cleaners may shorten the time it takes to pressure wash a driveway.

Q.How many square feet can you pressure wash in an hour?

A professional pressure washing company can cover around 1,000 square feet per hour, though the exact amount depends on the surface being washed, the condition of the surface, and the type of pressure washer being used.

Q.Do I need to seal my driveway after pressure washing?

It’s not necessary, but sealing after pressure washing can prevent stains from oil, grease, dirt, and mold, and it can prevent erosion over time.

Q. What do you spray on the driveway before pressure washing?
