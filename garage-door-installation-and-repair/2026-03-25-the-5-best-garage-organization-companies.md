What to Consider When Choosing One of the Best Garage Organization Companies

Before you hire a garage organization company, there are a few factors to consider. These can include the services and products offered, the company’s warranty, service area, availability, and consultation costs. Services

All of the recommended garage organization companies offer organization and storage solutions, but some offer additional services related to the garage area. These include garage repairs and enhancements like floor coating, garage door opener installation, garage door installation, weatherstripping, ventilation and exhaust fan installation, and light fixture installation and repair. Other companies offer luxury features like car lifts, tire racks, and workbench construction and installation to provide customers with the most organized garages possible. Some services are subject to state and local licensing specifications, so some services may not be available in certain locations. Products

Some professional garage organizers will recommend and provide custom cabinetry, shelving, and other proprietary garage organization systems. These types of custom products make the best use of the space and solve individual storage needs. Custom-made solutions can maximize the storage potential and improve the garage’s visual appeal. A homeowner can personalize the color, shape, material, and size of cabinetry, shelving, bin storage units, countertops, and door pulls. Overhead garage storage solutions can keep floor space open, and custom features like built-in workbenches can also maximize a garage’s usable space by transforming it into a DIY area. Additional accessories such as garage shelf organizers, drawer types, hinges, hardware, garage storage lockers, and expandable storage systems can also be personalized. However, bargain garage organizers may be sufficient if you don’t have a lot of things that need to remain in your garage. Plus, custom shelving and cabinetry will typically be more expensive than items that you can buy at a local home improvement store. Warranty

Many garage organization companies on our list offer some type of warranty. It’s important to note that just because there is a warranty, it doesn’t guarantee a product will automatically be fixed or replaced. A full warranty means that the company must replace or repair the product during the defined warranty period. This period can range anywhere from 30 to 90 days or can even extend for the life of the product. Even a full lifetime warranty comes with some limitations, though, so be sure to read the warranty to see exactly what it entails. Keep in mind that a full warranty doesn’t cover normal wear and tear.

A limited warranty is limited to the repair or replacement of specific parts or other restricted conditions. Many times, the warranty covers the cost of the parts and not the labor to repair the product. There can also be a stipulation that the manufacturer and the owner split the cost of repairs. Always read the fine print to determine exactly what is covered under the warranty for the product you buy. Service Area and Availability

Deciding on the best garage organization company while staying within your budget can be a bit of a challenge. Here are some frequently asked questions about garage organization companies to help guide you in your decisions.

Q. What is a garage organization company?

A garage organization company is a business that will come up with a plan to store your belongings and organize the garage. They will provide a storage solution that will best fit your needs and install the best garage organizer cabinets, shelving, bin systems, ceiling-mounted storage, slatwalls, workbenches, and more to fit your storage needs.

Q. How much does garage organization cost?

Garage organization can cost from $615 to $2,635, with the average around $1,455. Some professionals charge by the project or by the hour, with the average rate between $55 and $75 per hour. Custom-made storage solutions are typically the most expensive option.

Q. How do I organize my garage when I have a lot of stuff?

There are many garage storage ideas for homeowners to consider if they have a cluttered garage. It may also be worth getting rid of some items rather than trying to fit them all in a garage storage closet. It’s helpful to group your belongings into categories and decide if they need to be stored in the garage or if there is another place in your home that would make more sense. Donate items that are in good condition, and get rid of items that are in bad shape that you no longer need. Hire a professional to create a storage solution so everything has a place. This will make everything easier to find and create more free space to park the car or use as a work space.

Q. What should not be stored in a garage?

Certain items should never be stored in a garage, such as items that would attract bugs or rodents, valuable items, or hazardous materials. Items like pet food or birdseed could attract bugs, rodents, and animals, as the promise of a food source will entice them to make a cozy home in the garage. Valuable or fragile items may crack or warp due to extreme temperature fluctuations, and they could be at risk if the garage is a target for a break-in.
