Why make your bathroom eco-friendly? One of the most important reasons why is that you can improve the environment. This is the top reason why many choose to create a sustainable bathroom. Another good reason is economic benefits. Making your bathroom more sustainable can lower the cost of your water and electric bills. One of the biggest wastes in your bathroom will likely come from the toilet. Flushing traditional toilets can lose upwards of six gallons of water per flush. Installing an eco-friendly toilet can help cut down on water waste. A low-flow toilet, such as those with a WaterSense label, only uses about 1.6 gallons of water per flush. Such toilets can save upwards of $2900 over the lifetime of the toilet. This is better for the environment and your bank account. Replace Light Bulbs

Lighting is an essential part of any bathroom. Not only is it necessary for safely navigating the space, but it is useful when grooming. Since this light is used frequently, it is a good idea to incorporate LED bulbs like Shinestar LED bulbs for a vanity mirror. LED bulbs help save money and provide various lighting outputs for your space. For your bathroom, choosing a lightbulb output of about 7,000 to 8,000 lumens is ideal. Install a Low-Flow Showerhead

Since water loss is a significant source of energy waste in the bathroom, it is ideal for installing fixtures that reduce water use. Besides a low-flow toilet, you can also install a low-flow shower. You can choose a simple shower head like the Waterpik Shower Head or a more complex shower station like the Pulse Showerspas.

Low-flow showerheads come in both aerating and laminar types. Aerating showerheads use water mist to help reduce water flow, while laminar showerheads trickle water out in streams. The low pressure uses less water, which can help reduce water bills. Install Energy Star Water Heater

Water is an essential part of the bathroom. A great way to keep it at a comfortable temperature without using up too much energy is to get an Energy Star water heater. Such water heaters can save upwards of $3500 over the unit’s lifetime. Decorate with Natural Materials

When remodeling or decorating your bathroom, keep natural elements in mind. Incorporating such elements into your bathroom helps make it sustainable and creates a tranquil environment in your bathroom. If you want to integrate natural materials using decor, consider including woven baskets, sealed wood, and natural fiber rugs, such as jute or sisal. For shower curtains, consider incorporating ones made of hemp, cotton, or linen.

These are just a few ways that any homeowner can make their home more sustainable. Here are a few more honorable mentions for an eco-friendly bathroom you may want to include. Erin Little Windows

Not every bathroom has a window in it. But if your bathroom does, this could be a positive or a negative. On the one hand, you can welcome sunlight and fresh air by opening the window anytime. On the other hand, if the windows aren’t high-quality, this could be a source of losing energy. Replacing your window with Energy Star windows or even windows ideal for bathrooms, such as casement windows, could drastically reduce energy loss. Ventilation Fans

If you have a ventilation fan in your bathroom, this could be using plenty of energy each time you use it. To save money while eliminating moisture in your bathroom, you can invest in an Energy Star ventilation fan. Such ventilation systems produce less noise, use 50% less energy, and last longer than traditional systems. Heated Floors

If you want to save money on using your forced air heating system, you can install heated flooring to warm up your bathroom tile or wood floors. Heated floors, more commonly known as radiant floor heating, can be 30% more efficient than traditional HVAC, from heated flooring to warming up your bathroom.
