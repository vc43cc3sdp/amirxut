As American homes evolve, so do our functional spaces. The traditional powder room, once reserved for guests, is transforming into a versatile multi-use space. When designing your extra bathroom, try to make it functional and easy to maintain. By choosing the right materials and smart layouts, you can create a space that’s durable and stylish. Here are a few ideas:

Drying space: Install a permanent rod or pull-out clothesline in the shower area for air-drying delicate clothes or damp towels. This is a practical solution for big families or anyone who hand-washes their items.

Floor drains: Add an integral floor drain, especially if the bath is likely to see a lot of dirt or water. It helps prevent water buildup, keeping the space cleaner and safer.

Laundry and towel storage: Built-in storage for dirty clothes and towels helps keep things organized, especially if kids or gardeners will use the space. It also makes storing fresh linens convenient.

Lighting: Make sure you’ve got adequate lighting, especially in windowless bathrooms. Using a mix of ambient and task lighting makes the room bright and functional.

Ventilation: A strong exhaust fan is key to keeping the room fresh. It helps reduce moisture and odors, especially in bathrooms with high traffic or no windows.

Wainscoting: Protect your walls with durable materials like tongue-and-groove paneling, tile set level with the top of the backsplash, or even sheet stock such as Avonite, Corian, or other solid surfacing.
