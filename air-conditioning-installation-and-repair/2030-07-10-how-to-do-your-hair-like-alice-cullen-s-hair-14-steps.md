Love Alice's hair? Read on to find out how to do your hair like Alice Cullen from the Twilight series. Steps Method 1 Method 1 of 2: Short Pixie Haircuts Download Article 1

Get a short pixie-like haircut. Alice's pixie cut is about down to her chin but higher in some places. You can do this if your hair is a little longer—just make sure you have short layers. 2

[Your Hair](https://your-hair.northlist.xyz/your-hair/20260826.html)

[Hair Like Alice Cullen's](https://hair-like-alice-cullen-s.curblist.xyz/hair-like-alice-cullen-s/20260826783.html)

Wash your hair before you style it. Do this so you can get out the previous products in your hair. If you take a shower at night, blow dry your hair as usual (optional). Advertisement 3

[Cullen's Hair](https://cullen-s-hair.curblist.xyz/cullen-s-hair/202608260601.html)

Set up your straightener on HIGH or else it won't flip out right. 4

While your straightener is warming up, spray all your hair with a reliable hairspray—this will make it so the flip stays in place beforehand. Also spray your hair "upside down" and shake your hair lightly. 5

Once you have sprayed your hair, take your heated straightener and grab a section of your hair. Don't make the section too large! Take the straightener and curve outward and inward on the hair to make sure it flips out into styled disarray. 6

Repeat for each section of hair. Try to use very reliable hairspray and re-spray the side you started with first. Then, do the other side. Advertisement Method 2 Method 2 of 2: Thick Hair Download Article

If you have thick hair or want to have the Alice Cullen look everyday, follow these steps. They replicate the hair Alice had in New Moon in some scenes: 1

For the first time, wash your hair using good shampoo. 2 Dry your hair like normal. 3

[Cullen's Hair Steps](https://cullen-s-hair-steps.curblist.xyz/cullen-s-hair-steps/20260826039.html)

Get a hairdryer and either a small comb or a roller (curler) comb. 4

With the hairdryer on, wrap the end of your hair outwards around the comb, as if you are curling it. Don't do it too much or too high—otherwise, it will make it curl up too much. 5

With the hair still twisted around the comb, dry it with the hairdryer. 6

Do this with all your hair. If you have a fringe, put it to the left side 7

In the morning, it should still be sticking upwards. You might need to repeat this process once every few days. During the days you are not going to repeat the process, get a wet comb in the morning and twirl the bits that need twisting. 8 Finished.
