What to Consider When Choosing the Best PoE Security Camera Systems

Most PoE security camera systems include one or more PoE cameras as well as a network video recorder (NVR) to store the video footage. Stand-alone PoE cameras are also available for those who only need a single camera or for adding onto an existing system. The important features to keep in mind when choosing the best PoE security camera or camera system are explained ahead. Type

PoE ethernet cameras are available in the most popular security camera types, which include bullet, dome, turret, and pan-tilt-zoom (PTZ).

Bullet cameras, named for their cylindrical shape, are a basic and popular option. They have a fixed viewing angle and tend to offer great image quality. Plus, they obviously look like a camera, which by itself can make them a good visual deterrent against intruders.

Dome cameras, so called for their rounded housing made of break-resistant glass or plastic, also have a fixed viewing angle. Their small size and protective housing make them discreet and vandal-resistant, but the dome can cause glare, which can negatively impact image quality.

Turret cameras have a compact design and fixed viewing angle similar to that of a dome camera but without the glass housing. While this makes turret cameras less tamper-resistant, it also eliminates the image quality issues that can be caused by dome glass.

PTZ cameras are able to pan, tilt, and zoom, offering variable viewing angles. Users can move the camera’s field of view and zoom to see the area in more detail. Video Quality and Viewing Angle

PoE cameras can have excellent video quality with a resolution range from 1080p up to 4K. The higher the camera resolution, the more detailed the video image, which can mean the difference between identifying an intruder and useless footage. Most folks concerned about security will want a PoE camera with the best resolution they can afford.

The viewing angle, also called the field of view, refers to the visible area that the camera covers. The viewing angle can range from around 80 to a full 360 degrees. Some cameras also have a zoom feature, which allows users to zoom in on the camera’s field of view. Security Features

A security camera is only useful if it’s running properly, so ensure that the system is equipped for its environment. Weather-resistant construction is essential to ensure that an outdoor security camera will not be damaged by stormy conditions.

Look for a PoE external camera with an IP66 rating or higher. The IP (Ingress Protection) code measures how well protected a device is against dust and water intrusion. IP66 cameras are dustproof and protected against powerful water jets, meaning they’ll hold up well to bad weather or an accidental spray from the garden hose.

A vandal-resistant design is also an important feature for some homes and many businesses. A dome camera, with its break-resistant glass or plastic shield, will offer the best protection against tampering. Ease of Installation

One of the main draws of a PoE security camera system is its ease of installation. Since each camera only needs to connect to an ethernet cable, most systems feature a plug-and-play design where users simply connect the camera, the NVR, and the router to start monitoring and recording.

The power over ethernet camera systems make installation even easier on consumers with complete sets that include the NVR, power and installation cables, and mounting hardware. Q. What is a PoE security camera system?

A PoE security camera system utilizes PoE (Power over Ethernet) technology to power its IP cameras and transfer data. A PoE security camera draws power from and connects to the network via an ethernet cable. The same ethernet cable also feeds video footage back to the network for storage or remote viewing, creating a reliable and simple camera monitoring system.

Q. What type of cable do PoE cameras use?

PoE cameras use an ethernet cable that supports PoE to draw power and transmit data. Q. Is PoE better than Wi-Fi?

Both PoE security systems and Wi-Fi security systems have their distinct advantages. Those looking for a reliable, wired, and internet-enabled security camera system should consider a PoE system. Those looking for a wireless system would prefer a Wi-Fi security system. Q. Can I use PoE cameras at home?

Yes, PoE cameras are a great choice for home security. Q. How much power do PoE cameras use?

How much power a camera uses will depend on the camera (for example, a fixed camera tends to use less power than a PTZ camera). However, in general, PoE cameras don’t use very much power.

Q. Is there any possibility of a security system getting hacked?

Yes, it is possible for any security system that is connected to the internet to be hacked. However, the overall risk is low and many consumers feel the benefits of having a camera security system outweighs the small risk of being hacked.

Q. What’s the max distance for PoE cameras to work?

The maximum distance for most PoE cameras is 328 feet from the PoE injector or switch. Any further than this, the signal begins to weaken.
