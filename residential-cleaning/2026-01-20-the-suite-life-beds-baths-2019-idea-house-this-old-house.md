Today’s newly-constructed homes and remodels are designed to be airtight. The simple act of breathing overnight in these confined spaces can lead to rising C02 levels—and restless sleep. To combat the issue, this inconspicuous smart sensor on the nightstand continuously monitors indoor air quality, activating the ventilation system and purifying the air when needed.

HEALTHY HOME SYSTEM: Cosmos by Panasonic

Pro Tip: Ross Trethewey, This Old House home technology expert, recommends getting an indoor air quality monitor to take the guesswork out of what you’re breathing: “Knowledge is power. A monitor will let you know if your air quality is not that great, so you can do something about it.”

The stakes are higher than most homeowners realize: according to the EPA, indoor air can be up to 70% more polluted than the air outside, and people spend roughly 90% of their time indoors—where harmful pollutants can run 2 to 5 times higher than outdoor levels. A bedside monitor and an automated ventilation response are simple ways to flip those odds. See-Through Fireplace with a View Photo by Nat Rea
