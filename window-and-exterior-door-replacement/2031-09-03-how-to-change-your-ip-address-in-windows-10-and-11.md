This guide will teach you how to change both your public and private IP addresses on a Windows computer. Your public IP address is the address that your computer broadcasts to other networks, while your private IP address is your computer's specific address within your own wireless network. Changing either of these may fix connection issues. Changing Your IP Address in Windows

Reset your public IP by power-cycling your router, then disabling and re-enabling Wi-Fi.

Manually set your private IP address in Windows 11 and Windows 10 by going to your network settings.

Renew a private IP address in Command Prompt with "ipconfig /release" and "ipconfig /renew" to fix IP-related issues. Steps Section 1 of 5: Resetting a Public IP Download Article 1

Unplug your router and modem. Most service providers assign dynamic IPs, which change from time to time. By disconnecting your modem for a long period of time, it's possible that you will get a new address assigned to you when you turn it back on.

You may want to check your current IP address before doing this.

Simply unplugging the router and modem from the wall will work.

ISPs have different policies regarding how often they'll change users' IP addresses. Sometimes IP addresses change daily, and others may only change them every few weeks. 2

Manually Setting a Private IP (Windows 11) Download Article 1

Open the Settings app. The easiest way to do this is to press ⊞ Win+i on your keyboard. 2

Click Network & internet in the left-hand sidebar. It's got a blue icon that looks like a Wi-Fi signal icon. 3

Choose your connection type. If you connect via Wi-Fi, click on Wi-Fi, then click Manage known networks. If you connect via a cable, click Ethernet. 4

Click Edit next to IP assignment. It will either say "Automatic (DHCP)" or "Manual." 5

Choose Manual in the drop-down, if it's not already selected. This will let you change your IP address yourself. 6

Click the toggle next to IPv4 if it's not already toggled on. This will cause a number of fields to appear. 7

Enter your desired IP settings. Here's what each field means:

IP address: Usually, your device's IP address will be "192.168.1.X" (or a similar set of numbers), wherein "X" is specific to your device. Change the "X" value to a number between 1 and 100. Make sure you don't use the same IP address as another device on your network (e.g., your phone).

Subnet prefix length: This depends on your IP address, but will usually be "255.255.255.X".

Gateway: This is your router's IP address.

Preferred DNS: Your preferred DNS address (for example: "208.67.222.222" for the OpenDNS server, or "8.8.8.8" for Google's server).

Why would I change my public IP versus my private IP? You'd change your public IP address in the event that you were kicked out of a gaming service (e.g., Steam), while you'd change your private IP address to fix website loading errors. 2

[Address in](https://address-in.curblist.xyz/address-in/202608267758.html)

Does using a proxy or VPN change my IP? Using a proxy server or a VPN will not change your IP address in the traditional sense, but it will route your internet traffic through a server with a different IP address. This makes your IP address display as that server's IP address to websites and services outside of your network. 3

Can I change my IP through my browser? You can't change your IP in your browser, but you can use the browser called Tor to hide your IP address while browsing. However, this browser can be dangerous and very slow to use, as it allows you to access the dark web. 4
